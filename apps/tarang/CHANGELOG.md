# Tarang changelog

## 1.3.0 · 27 Nov 2026
- New first-launch welcome and step-by-step setup (music access, notifications, folders to skip, theme).
- New full player: "Now playing" top bar, round buttons, cover carousel (with an optional peek of the next cover), round heart, thicker wavy seek bar, joined ⏮ ⏯ ⏭ that squish when pressed, Sleep · Lyrics · Playlist toolbar.
- A live lyric line follows the music for songs with synced lyrics; the bottom strip shows what plays next and opens Up next.
- Lyrics page: add a .lrc file or type lyrics, save lyrics into the song file, and an "Always save lyrics" setting.
- Swipe down to the mini player is fixed: the cover never gets cut off or tilted mid-way, and a short pull finishes the move.
- One queue. Tapping a song plays that list (with Undo); Play next moves a song that is already queued; Save queue as playlist; Clear queue.
- Real shuffle: Up next shows the true shuffled order, and turning it off brings back the earlier order. New "Keep shuffle on" setting. Shuffle and repeat are remembered.
- Material 3 Expressive look: collapsing titles, shimmer while loading, dancing bars on the playing song, grouped Settings with a haptics switch.
- Stats moved into Settings; the dock has Home · Folders · Library and sits level with the mini player, tinted with the song's colour.
- Select many songs and act on them from a floating toolbar, including deleting them.
- Smoother app icon.

## 1.2.0 · 22 Nov 2026
- Swiping left/right on the cover now always changes the song; the new cover slides in once that song has loaded.
- Swipe down on the full player and swipe up on the mini player never stop halfway: once you swipe and let go, it completes.
- Swiping on the mini player stays inside the mini player's rounded shape.

## 1.1.0 · 22 Nov 2026
- Swipe down anywhere on the full player (cover, title, buttons, bottom strip) to close it; it follows your finger.
- Swipe left/right on the cover: it slides out with your finger and the next song's cover slides in.
- Play and pause morph into each other, and the play button turns round when paused and a rounded square when playing.
- The round cover in the mini player spins like a record while music plays.
- Shuffle and repeat now sit in one evenly spaced row with previous, play and next; a dot shows when they're on.
- The bottom of the full player shows only a handle and "Up next · song"; pull up for Up next, Lyrics and Info.
- Bottom bar: the press effect stays inside the pill, and the pill grows in when you switch tabs.
- Fixed: the faded mini player at the top of the full player, which made ⋮ act as play/pause.
- Fixed: artist name squeezed out or shown with a stray dot.
- Fixed: MP3s from downloaders showing the wrong length and seeming stuck at the end.

## 1.0.0 · 22 Nov 2026
First version of the new Tarang, rebuilt from scratch.
- Mini player that is its own progress bar and morphs into the full player with your finger.
- Full player in the song's cover colour: wavy seek bar, Sleep, Speed, Sound and Playlist sheets that slide up from the bottom.
- Up next (3 saved queues), real lyrics (.lrc files, lyrics inside the song, or typed in) and real file info.
- Home with mixes, Folders, Library (songs, albums, artists, playlists), Stats from real listening time.
- Equalizer, bass boost and surround inside the app; edit song details and covers; delete songs for real.
- Home-screen widget, notification controls, lock-screen cover, resume on headphones.
- Fully offline: no internet permission.
