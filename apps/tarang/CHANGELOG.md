# Tarang changelog

## 1.1.0 · 22 Nov 2026
- Swipe down anywhere on the full player (cover, title, buttons, bottom strip) to close it; it follows your finger.
- Swipe left/right on the cover: it slides out with your finger and the next song's cover slides in.
- Play and pause morph into each other, and the play button turns round when paused and a rounded square when playing.
- The round cover in the mini player spins like a record while music plays.
- Shuffle and repeat now sit in one evenly spaced row with previous, play and next; a dot shows when they're on.
- The bottom of the full player shows only a handle and "Up next · song"; pull up for Up next, Lyrics and Info.
- Bottom bar: the press effect stays inside the pill, and the pill grows in when you switch tabs.
- Fixed: the faded mini player at the top of the full player, which made ⋮ act as play/pause.
- Fixed: artist name squeezed out or shown with a stray dot.
- Fixed: MP3s from downloaders showing the wrong length and seeming stuck at the end.

## 1.0.0 · 22 Nov 2026
First version of the new Tarang, rebuilt from scratch.
- Mini player that is its own progress bar and morphs into the full player with your finger.
- Full player in the song's cover colour: wavy seek bar, Sleep, Speed, Sound and Playlist sheets that slide up from the bottom.
- Up next (3 saved queues), real lyrics (.lrc files, lyrics inside the song, or typed in) and real file info.
- Home with mixes, Folders, Library (songs, albums, artists, playlists), Stats from real listening time.
- Equalizer, bass boost and surround inside the app; edit song details and covers; delete songs for real.
- Home-screen widget, notification controls, lock-screen cover, resume on headphones.
- Fully offline: no internet permission.
