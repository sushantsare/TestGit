# PageOne changelog

## 27 Nov 2026 · 1.0.1
Smoother zoom: pages zoom for real while you pinch and stay at that zoom when you lift your fingers, then turn sharp. Double-tap steps closer and back to fit around the spot you tapped. A pinch is never taken for a tap. One-page PDFs and pages smaller than the screen sit in the middle. The sort arrow lines up with Name / Date / Size, and list screens keep a solid strip behind the status bar. Tablets show one page per screen when turned sideways too; two pages side by side is now a switch in Reading options (off). With the phone sideways, Home, Recent and Starred all keep the same side rail.

## 27 Nov 2026 · 1.0.0
First version: Home · Recent · Starred, every PDF on the phone by folder, fast reader (find, select and copy, highlight, pen, notes, forms, reflow, crop margins, night/sepia, read aloud, bookmarks, contents), merge PDFs & images, split, compress, duplicate finder, app lock, fully offline.
